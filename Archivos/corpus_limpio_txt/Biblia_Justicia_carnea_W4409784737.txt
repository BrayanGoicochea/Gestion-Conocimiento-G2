Impact of Fermentation on Amino Acid Profile of Cold and Hot Water Extracts of Justicia carnea Leaves

Jeroh, P. and Achuba, F.I.

Department of Biochemistry, Delta State University Abraka.

Coresponding Author: jerohpatience@gmail.com

Abstract

This study investigates the impact of fermentation on the amino acid profile of Justicia carnea

leaves extracts and the fermented must of the hot water extract. The cold water extract (C)

(25 0 C) , hot water extract (H) at 10 0 c and the fermented must (W) us ing bakers yeast ( Sacharamyces cerevisiae ) were analyzed with High - Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) to determine changes in amino acid concentrations. The results show that fermentation significantly (p<0.05) alters the amino acid composition, wit h increases in key amino acids such as L - Proline, L - Serine, and L - Leucine relative to the hot water and cold water extracts. These findings highlight the potential of fermentation to enhance the amino acid profile of J . carnea water extract, a plant known f or its medicinal properties.

Keywords

Justicia carnea , fermentation, amino acids, hot water extract, nutrition.

Introduction

Justicia carnea which is the largest genus of the family of plants known as Acanthaceae , is a medicinal plant known for its bioactive properties, some local names include Ewueje by the yorubas and ogwuobara by the Ibos. it is comonly known as jacobina, brazilian plume, etc (Onyeabo et al ., 2017; Anarado et al. , 2021,). It is traditionaly used for its potential in improving blod levels, obesity control, treatment of malaria, diabetes, blod presure reduction and suporting overal health (Okwu and Josiah 206; Olayinka & Adesina, 2020; Igbinovia et al ., 2020; Anigboro et al.,

2021a; Anigboro et al., 2021b; Ajuru et al ., 2021; Andrew et a., 2024). Various studies have shown that justicia carnea

is rich in phytochemicals and antioxidants, which includes; flavonoids, phenols, tanins, saponins alkaloids and cardiac glycosides (Akinmoladun et al ., 2016; Adebayo et al . 2018; Ogunyemi et al ., 2020). Amino acids, the building blocks of proteins, play a critical role in celular function and health maintenance. They are involved in the regulation of key metabolic pathways and proceses that are crucial for the growth and the maintenance of organisms. (Cesari et al ., 205; Wu, 209).

Fermentation, a biochemical proces involving microrganisms, is known to improve the bioavailability of nutrients in plant extracts by breaking down complex molecules. (Adams, 190; Holzapfel 195; Gagia, et al.,201; Rizelo et al .,2016). Fermentation has ben shown to break down complex plant molecules, making nutrients more bio - available and potentialy altering the amino acid profile (Azat et al ., 2016).

Materials and Methods:

Colection and Cleaning of Plant Leaf

Fresh J . carnea leaves were localy colected from a nearby bush in Abraka, Ethiope East Local Governmen t Area of Delta State and authenticated by a taxonomist, Prof, Joseph Ebiguai of the Dept. of Botany, Faculty of biological Science, University of Calabar, with Voucher number: UC - BOT/038 The leaves were weighed and cleaned by rinsing in clean potable wate r.

Preparation of Justiciacarnea extracts

Cold extract preparation

Ten grams of J . carnea leaves were freshly harvested, weighed and washed with tap water respectively. The weighed leaves were then transfered into a mortar and crushed under cold ice. 10 mL (1 litre) of distiled water was utilized for the homogenization, the extract was then filtered through chese cloth with fine pore, and the filtrate was filtered for the second time using Whatman No. 1 filter paper. The resulting filtrat e was then used for the study. Storage of filtrate was done in a refrigerator at 4 0 C (Sahira and Catherine, 2015).

Hot extract preparation

The decoction of hot water (10%w/v) of the J . carnea was prepared by weighing a freshly washed 10 gram of the leaves and boiled with 10ml of distiled water for 10 mins under a mild heat condition. The extract was obtained from a two - step filtration employed by chese cloth and Whatman No. 1 filter paper respectively. The filtrate obtained was stored in refrigerator at 4 0 C. (Sahira and Catherine, 2015).

Fermentation of hot water extract

Fiften litres (15 L) of the hot water extract was fermented for 9 days. The fermentation vesel was washed and sterilized with a weak bleach solution and rinsed with plenty of water. Aluminium hydrogen sulfite (Al(HSO 3 ) 3 ) was aded in the ratio of 0.14 g/L to prevent the growth of wild strains of bacteria/ fungi. (Gutierez, et al ., 2017). The must for fermentation was prepared by ading sugar in the ratio of 10 g/L (making a total weight of 1.5 Kg.) to the hot water extract of J. carnea . and inoculated with a fermentation broth prepared from scratch as described below.
